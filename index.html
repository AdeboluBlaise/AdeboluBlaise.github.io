Selected work

A few pieces I can share publicly. Most of my analysis is client work and stays private.

Food prices in Nigeria

Ten years of monthly prices for nine staples. I modelled how the prices move together with a vector autoregression and Granger causality tests.

3.6×rise in the price of imported rice, 2012 to 2022

Code and plots (R)

Insurance fraud

15,419 vehicle insurance claims. I used chi-square tests to find which claim features go with fraud, then built the dashboard and wrote up the findings.

6%of claims were fraudulent, 923 in all

Findings (slides)Dashboard

Hospital resources and outcomes

35 hospitals: how beds, staff and deaths relate. The data weren’t normally distributed, so I used rank-based tests alongside t-tests and confidence intervals.

0.85rank correlation between beds and staff

Code and plots (R)

Governance and economic growth

Does good governance go with growth? Multiple regression on 885 country-years, with checks for collinearity and for the model’s assumptions.

134countries, 2012 to 2021

Code (R)

Predicting phone prices

Predicting a phone’s price band from 20 specifications. I compared three models on phones they hadn’t seen; RAM was the strongest predictor.

96.5%accuracy of the best model
